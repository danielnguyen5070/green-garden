A short video from [Ngọc Ngân Bến Tre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Y0WtFtJTeM) shows why **neem (sầu đâu)** remains a multipurpose favorite among Vietnamese nurseries.

## What do the seedlings look like?

In the video, the seller introduces a healthy batch of neem seedlings ready to leave the nursery.

- Height measured **from the pot rim up**
- Plants about **four tấc** tall (around 40 cm)
- Strong plants, available in quantity

A useful tip when ordering: ask whether height is measured from the pot rim or from the soil surface in the bag.

## Everyday uses mentioned in the video

The focus is household usefulness more than ornamental planting.

### 1. Leaves for salad

Young leaves can be used in **salad**. As with any edible plant, choose clean, tender leaves and wash thoroughly before cooking.

### 2. Flowers for stir-fry

The presenter highlights that **neem flowers** make a delicious stir-fry. Seasonal blooms are another reason people grow this tree beyond shade and foliage.

### 3. Leaf baths for sensitive skin

Leaves are also boiled for bathing water — traditionally associated with soothing **heat rash** in children and sensitive skin. This is home-care folklore from the video, not medical advice — use gently with young children and seek professional guidance when needed.

### 4. Bitter medicinal reputation of the trunk

The video also mentions the **bitter medicinal reputation** of neem wood. It is a familiar plant in folk life; many people grow it for shade and household uses together.

## Why it fits Ngoc Ngan Ben Tre readers

If you are choosing seedlings for a home garden, neem is worth considering because it combines:

- a compact starting size that ships easily
- kitchen potential (leaves and flowers)
- traditional leaf-bath / skin-care habits
- the character of a practical, familiar useful tree

## Tips for first-time growers

The video mainly introduces seedlings, but a few practical points still apply:

1. **Pick healthy plants** — firm stems, good green leaves, no wilting.
2. **Confirm how height is measured** — from the pot rim or the bag surface.
3. **Plan for mature size** — seedlings are small, but neem becomes a large tree.
4. **Use with care** — food prep or leaf baths should stay clean, gentle, and informed.

## Closing

Neem is more than “a green plant for looks.” In this nursery intro, it is framed as a **useful household tree**: salad leaves, tasty stir-fried flowers, leaf baths for sensitive skin, and a long-standing bitter-medicine reputation.

If you want a practical garden — edible, familiar, and rooted in Vietnamese everyday life — neem seedlings are worth learning more about.
